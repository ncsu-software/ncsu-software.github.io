    <h3 class="subheading">Before you apply</h3>
    <div class="faq">
      <details>
        <summary>When do I need to apply?</summary>
        <p>American universities admit new graduate students to start in mid-August or mid-January, and applications open months before those dates. Plan your application at least a year before you want to start.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>Can a professor admit me directly?</summary>
        <p>No. Admission decisions are made by the department, so please review the admissions procedures on the <a href="https://www.csc.ncsu.edu/academics/graduate/">departmental website</a>. Once you are admitted, faculty can supervise you. During the review, faculty see the list of candidates and may flag a few names, so if you plan to apply, let the faculty member you would like to work with know.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>How should I write to a professor?</summary>
        <p>Don't send a form letter; we receive plenty of those. Show that you have done your homework: mention their current research, perhaps one of their recent papers, and how your own work or interests connect to it.</p>
        <p>Be specific about your plans, for example: "I am preparing an application for the January intake, which I will submit to the department."</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>Who do I contact with other questions?</summary>
        <p>Email <a href="mailto:csc_gradadmissions@ncsu.edu">csc_gradadmissions@ncsu.edu</a> and the graduate office will do its best to help.</p>
      </details>
    </div>
