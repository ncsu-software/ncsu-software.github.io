How to get involved

For students

Do research with us

  1. Browse our research themes and the labs and publications behind them.
  2. Find a faculty member whose work excites you among our researchers, and reach out directly.
  3. Thinking about grad school? See For Students or email csc_gradadmissions@ncsu.edu.

For industry

Innovate with us

  1. See how we partner with companies and agencies on For Industry.
  2. Bring us a hard problem: match it to a research theme above and contact that faculty member.
  3. Recruit our graduates; read where they are now in Success Stories.
